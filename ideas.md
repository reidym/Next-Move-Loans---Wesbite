# Next Move Loans — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches considered

### Theme Name: Pathfinder Editorial

**Very Brief Intro:** A confident editorial system influenced by modernist wayfinding, premium magazines, and civic signage. Directional lines, numbered stages, deep navy fields, paper warmth, and coral interruptions make the brand feel strategic, human, and always in motion.

**Probability:** 0.037

### Theme Name: Warm Modern Homestead

**Very Brief Intro:** An image-led, regional-lifestyle direction combining natural light, tactile paper textures, wide landscape crops, and calm architectural typography. It would feel grounded and approachable, but less challenger-led.

**Probability:** 0.082

### Theme Name: Precision Workshop

**Very Brief Intro:** A sharper neo-industrial system inspired by plans, measurement marks, machinery labels, and technical diagrams. It would suit business and asset finance strongly, but risk feeling colder across home and family decisions.

**Probability:** 0.019

## Chosen approach: Pathfinder Editorial

### Design Movement

The chosen direction combines **International Typographic Style**, contemporary editorial design, and public wayfinding systems. It uses disciplined typography and spatial rhythm, then introduces human warmth through the approved paper, coral, and gold palette.

### Core Principles

1. **Direction is visible.** Every major section should make the next step easy to understand through pathways, arrows, numbers, and clear transitions.
2. **Clarity earns trust.** Complex topics are presented with strong hierarchy, short copy blocks, generous space, and practical labels rather than decorative clutter.
3. **Strategy comes before product.** The interface prioritises the belief system and The Approval Method™ before exposing the service taxonomy.
4. **Warmth prevents finance from feeling institutional.** Human imagery, paper-toned surfaces, coral interventions, and conversational language balance the precision of the grid.

### Color Philosophy

**Dark Blue `#16203A`** is the structural colour: it signals intelligence, confidence, and long-term thinking. **Paper `#F7F5F1`** replaces clinical white to create warmth and editorial tactility. **Coral `#EC7354`** is the unmistakable action colour, used for decisive CTAs, progress markers, and the visual idea of forward movement. **Slate `#4C5566`** supports long-form readability, while **Gold `#C3A342`** is reserved for opportunity, milestones, and strategic accents rather than everyday decoration.

### Layout Paradigm

The site uses an **offset editorial spine** rather than a conventional sequence of centred rows. Desktop sections alternate between narrow text columns, wide image planes, vertical index labels, and full-bleed statement bands. A thin directional line creates continuity between sections. Mobile keeps the same hierarchy but turns the spine into a left-edge route marker, preserving intention rather than merely stacking desktop cards.

### Signature Elements

1. **The Moving Line:** a thin coral-to-gold pathway with nodes, arrowheads, and controlled bends that connects process steps and section transitions.
2. **Build Marks:** oversized outlined words, crop marks, small coordinates, and numbered labels that make the site feel like a plan being brought to life.
3. **Question Interruptions:** dark-blue editorial bands containing one sharp question or challenger statement, often split by a coral directional glyph.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel **decisive and calm**. Buttons compress subtly on activation. Navigation panels open quickly from the point of action. Links reveal a short directional underline rather than glowing or bouncing. Cards are used selectively and should shift with a slight physical lift only when the entire surface is actionable.

### Animation

Motion reinforces progress rather than spectacle. Entrance transitions use opacity with a small 10–18px directional translation and a 180–260ms ease-out. Process nodes reveal in a 50ms stagger. The Moving Line may draw once as it enters the viewport, but it must be disabled for reduced-motion users. Hover transitions remain under 180ms. No looping decorative animation, parallax, or layout-shifting effects are permitted.

### Typography System

The approved **Figtree** family remains canonical. Titles use Figtree Bold with tight tracking and editorial line breaks. Section headings use Figtree Bold or Medium, while body copy uses Figtree Regular with generous leading. Small navigation, eyebrows, and metadata use Figtree Medium in uppercase with expanded tracking. Scale should be dramatic but disciplined: desktop display text reaches 72–96px where copy length permits, while body text remains 17–19px for comfortable reading.

### Brand Essence

**Positioning:** Next Move Loans is the modern Australian finance brokerage for people building something bigger, combining clarity, strategy, and the right lending support so the loan serves the plan—not the other way around.

**Personality:** Confident. Curious. Momentum-building.

### Brand Voice

Headlines challenge an assumption or open a possibility. CTAs sound like the next useful action, not a sales funnel. Microcopy is candid about what happens next and avoids generic broker promises.

**Example headline:** “The bank can tell you how much you can borrow. That doesn’t mean it can tell you the right move.”

**Example CTA microcopy:** “Bring the half-formed idea. We’ll help you turn it into a plan.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The supplied Next Move Loans logo assets are the canonical identity. The coral directional icon should appear as a confident navigation marker and favicon, while the reversed horizontal wordmark is preferred on dark blue. The logo must retain approved spacing and proportions; it should never be recreated with live text or displayed at token size.

### Signature Brand Color

**Next Move Coral — `#EC7354`** is the ownable brand colour. It should represent the moment uncertainty becomes action.

## Style Decisions

- The hero will be asymmetric: editorial copy on the left, a custom pathway-based visual on the right, and a trust strip integrated into the lower edge.
- Rounded corners will be restrained and varied by purpose; large image planes and statement bands may use clipped or square geometry.
- No purple gradients, glassmorphism, generic financial stock-photo mosaics, or Inter typography.
- Visual energy will come from composition, direction, scale, and contrast—not decorative effects.
- The Moving Line must remain visible as a continuous route system on every major page through connected ticks, nodes, arrows, bends, or numbered markers—not only inside process modules.
- Sibling internal pages must vary at least one major editorial composition, such as the hero plane, statement band, route board, vertical label, or oversized build mark, while retaining the same brand language.
- Directory surfaces such as service lists, article indexes, and related-content areas must read as wayfinding boards with numbered routes and directional coordinates rather than neutral card grids.
