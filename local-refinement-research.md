# Local refinement research

## Warragul

Baw Baw Shire Council maintains both an economic profile and a community profile intended to describe the area’s role in the broader economy and explore local strengths. The council page also identifies a customer-service presence at the West Gippsland Arts Centre in Warragul. The location page should therefore speak to Warragul as a genuine service area with connected household, construction, self-employed, trade, business and regional-growth decisions, while avoiding unverified property-price or population claims.

Source: [Baw Baw Shire Council — Economic Profile](https://www.bawbawshire.vic.gov.au/Your-Business/Economic-Profile)

## Berwick

The Australian Bureau of Statistics recognises Berwick as a 2021 Census **Suburb and Locality** with area code SAL20224 and provides General, First Nations, Time Series and Working Population community profiles for that geography. The website should present Berwick as a real town/suburb service area and an authentic part of Martin Reidy’s business origin story. It should not imply a current physical office or use census statistics without extracting and attributing the relevant table.

Source: [Australian Bureau of Statistics — 2021 Berwick Census Community Profiles](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/community-profiles/2021/SAL20224)

## Implementation rule

Local pages will use durable, decision-relevant context rather than market forecasts, median-price claims or generic town descriptions. A page may describe the finance situations the team genuinely handles in that area, distinguish a confirmed physical office from a service area, and link to relevant solution and loan-type pages. Any new numerical claim must be verified and dated before publication.

## Leongatha

South Gippsland Shire Council’s community-statistics page identifies Leongatha as the most populous town in the shire in its published town comparison, recording 5,869 residents and 3.8% growth from 2016 in the cited profile. The council also links community, population, economic and housing profiles for ongoing reference. This supports positioning Leongatha as the confirmed physical office and a practical local hub for connected home, acreage, construction, self-employed, business and asset-finance decisions. The website should cite or date the numerical profile if it is displayed; the public page can use the more durable “largest town in the council’s published comparison” wording without making a market forecast.

Source: [South Gippsland Shire Council — Shire and Community Statistics](https://www.southgippsland.vic.gov.au/homepage/125/shire_and_community_statistics)
